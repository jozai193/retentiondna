# RetentionDNA demo — two-minute recording script

## 00:00–00:08 — The problem

**On screen:** RetentionDNA workspace, full page.

**Voiceover:** “Retention graphs show where viewers leave, but creators still have to guess why. RetentionDNA turns that evidence into a reviewable edit.”

## 00:08–00:20 — The input

**On screen:** Open **Upload project**; point to video, retention file, and transcript inputs, then close the dialog and keep the built-in sample.

**Voiceover:** “Load a draft, a retention CSV or saved YouTube Analytics response, and an optional transcript. The files stay in the browser.”

## 00:20–00:38 — The signal

**On screen:** Click the high-impact dip at 00:25. Show the video frame, curve marker, evidence cards, and aligned transcript.

**Voiceover:** “Here, the audience drops 30.5 points at 25 seconds. Each card says whether its evidence was measured, timestamp-aligned, or unavailable. The sample contains measured silence and scene changes.”

## 00:38–00:52 — The recommendation

**On screen:** Scroll or focus the right panel. Point to the learned rule and repair.

**Voiceover:** “RetentionDNA separates measured history from its recommendation. It explains the editing rule, proposes a bounded cut, and never promises that retention will improve.”

## 00:52–01:08 — Before and after

**On screen:** Click **Preview**, then **Create better cut**. Show the before/after duration cards and let playback cross the repair window.

**Voiceover:** “The non-destructive preview removes 12 to 28 seconds, bridging the promise directly into the first useful step while preserving the source.”

## 01:08–01:23 — Replay promotion

**On screen:** Select the replay spike at 01:15 and create its better cut. Let the teaser play and jump to the beginning.

**Voiceover:** “A replay spike uses a different operation: RetentionDNA promotes a bounded teaser from that moment, then returns to the full source.”

## 01:23–01:38 — Auditable output

**On screen:** Click **Export edit plan** and briefly show `artifacts/sample-plan.json` beside the rendered MP4.

**Voiceover:** “The export binds every edit to the exact source using its size, duration, and SHA-256. The renderer rejects unknown actions or the wrong video.”

## 01:38–01:50 — Learning loop

**On screen:** Point to **Compare published result** and **Creator DNA**.

**Voiceover:** “A later curve can be compared at the same moment, while Creator DNA keeps only small pattern summaries on this device—not videos or transcripts.”

## 01:50–01:58 — Close

**On screen:** RetentionDNA logo and selected signal.

**Voiceover:** “RetentionDNA: stop guessing at the next edit. Learn from what your audience actually watched.”

## Recording notes

- Record at 1920×1080, browser zoom 100%, notifications off.
- Keep the pointer slow and deliberate; do not linger on upload dialogs.
- Use the built-in sample so the take is deterministic.
- Export at 1080p H.264, 30 fps, with clear voice at roughly -14 LUFS.
- Target 112–118 seconds; stay under two minutes unless the Devpost form states otherwise.
